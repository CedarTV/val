Val support

Start with a sample story, or bring a book of your own.

Val is in development. A limited, invitation-only beta is planned after Apple review. Google Drive is optional and is not yet Google-verified.

Getting started

Open a sample story on your Nook, or choose Library → Add books → Import from Files. You can also add a print book to track your reading.

Val supports DRM-free EPUB, PDF, UTF-8 text, FB2, and supported MOBI editions, plus MP3, M4B, M4A, AAC, WAV, AIFF, and FLAC audio. Protected store purchases, KF8/AZW3, HUFF/CDIC MOBI, and interactive or fixed-layout EPUB are not supported.

Reading and notes

Choose Read from a book's details. Reading appearance changes type, paper, and page or scrolling mode. Select text to highlight or add a sticky. Find your notes in Journal or the book's Stickies & highlights collection; Return to passage reopens the saved location.

Listening

Import audio from Files. For a book with multiple tracks, select its files together and review the order. Use playback speed, chapters, skip controls, and a sleep timer. Pair text and audio editions from Book actions. Read-along preparation needs downloaded audio and supported on-device speech recognition; alignment may leave unmatched passages.

Connections

Audiobookshelf requires your own server account. Google Drive imports files you choose with read-only access. During this limited beta, Google displays an unverified-app consent warning and limits the project to 100 authorizing users in total. Connect only if you are comfortable granting the displayed permissions. Your organization or Google Advanced Protection may block access. You can still use sample stories and import through Files without connecting Google Drive. Folder scans do not run at guaranteed times because iOS manages background work; use Scan now and keep Val open for large imports.

Optional iCloud sync shares reading state and notes across devices using the same Apple account. Import matching book files on each device; book files themselves do not transfer through reading-state sync.

Back up your library

In Settings, choose Export a complete Val backup before changing devices. Restoring replaces the current library, so save a backup first. Credentials are excluded. Storage controls can remove downloaded audio separately from its book record.

Get in touch

Email tyler.bigwin@outlook.com. Include your app version, iOS version, file format, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send credentials or book files you do not have permission to share.

Settings → Help & support includes feature guides and a redacted diagnostic preview. Review diagnostics before sending them. If testing through TestFlight, you can also use TestFlight's feedback controls.
