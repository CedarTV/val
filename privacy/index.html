Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · Publisher: Tyler Bigwin

Val is a personal reading app for iPhone. You can read local books without creating a Val account. Val has no advertising or analytics SDKs and no developer-operated library-hosting backend.

Information on your device

Val stores imported books and audio, cover and spine artwork, book details, reading and listening positions, notes, highlights, reviews, reading history, and preferences in its local library. A name you enter is used within the app. Server credentials and Google authorization tokens are stored in iOS Keychain, separate from Val library backups.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. Google Sign-In provides your account identity and authorization tokens. Val uses your name, email address, and profile information to identify the connected account. It requests read-only Drive access to list existing files and folders, display file metadata, and download supported books and audio you select. When you select a watched folder, Val can scan it and import newly discovered supported files, including subfolders if enabled.

Drive requests and downloads go directly between your device and Google. Val does not send Google authorization tokens or Drive file contents to a developer-operated server. Val does not edit or delete your Drive files. It stores imported files, account identity, selected sources, import receipts, and scan results locally. Those library records and imported media are included if you choose to export a complete Val backup; authorization tokens are not.

Val uses Google user data only to provide the visible account connection, file browsing, import, and reading features you choose. Val does not sell this data, use it for advertising, use it to train general-purpose AI models, or provide developers with access to your Drive. Val's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You may choose to export or share imported content using system controls, or use other optional features described below. The destination you select receives the content you export. Google's handling of data is described in Google's privacy policy.

Other services you choose

iCloud. When enabled, Val sends reading and listening state, notes, highlights, and preferences to your private CloudKit database. Reading-state sync does not upload book or audio files, server credentials, or Google authorization tokens. Apple handles iCloud under its own privacy policy.

Audiobookshelf. Val connects to the server address you provide to authenticate, browse and play your library, download selected audio, and synchronize progress when enabled. Your server operator may log requests. Val does not send your private reading notes to Audiobookshelf.

Book matching. When you request online matching, Val sends search terms, identifiers, region, and filters to the selected provider: Audible, Google Books, Open Library, Apple Books, FantLab, Audiobook Covers, or your custom provider. Audible details may also contact Audnexus. Cover images come from provider-supplied hosts. Book files, reading history, and notes are not uploaded for matching. Provider keys and authorization headers stay in device Keychain and are excluded from library backups.

Permissions, backups, and sharing

Val accesses files and photos you select through system pickers. It asks to save images when you choose that action. Read-along preparation uses on-device speech recognition to match downloaded audio to text; Val does not upload that audio for transcription. System resources such as speech voices are managed by iOS.

Library backups, reading cards, and diagnostic exports are created when you request them. Diagnostics can be previewed and redact credentials. Review exported content before sharing it. System-managed device backups may also retain app data according to your Apple settings.

Retention and deletion

Imported books and library records remain on your device until you remove them. Disconnecting Google Drive revokes Val's Google grant when online and clears the connection; already imported books remain available until you delete them. You can also remove Val's access in your Google Account connections.

Remove books, delete notes, disconnect services, or turn off sync in Val. Disabling iCloud sync does not delete previously synced cloud records; use Apple's iCloud storage controls for cloud data. Removing Val removes its app-container data. Keychain items and system backups may have separate retention behavior. Copies you export or share remain at their destinations until you remove them.

This website and beta feedback

This website uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information under the GitHub privacy statement. If you email support, the information you send is used to respond and troubleshoot. Avoid sending passwords, access tokens, or private book files. TestFlight feedback and crash reports are handled through Apple's beta-testing service when you choose to test with TestFlight.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent to support, contact Tyler Bigwin at tyler.bigwin@outlook.com. This policy will be updated if Val's data handling changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.
